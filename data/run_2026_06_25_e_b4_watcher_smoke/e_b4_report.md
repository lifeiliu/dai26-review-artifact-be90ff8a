## Interpretation

E-B4 supports the root-quorum plus STH cross-observation story: below-threshold roots fail certificate verification, while quorum-certified split views are detected after honest observers compare same-size STHs. The watcher-coverage extension measures how strongly this depends on observer count, reporting coverage, observation interval, and selective disclosure.
